🌱 Sustainability Tips

Actionable recommendations to reduce your carbon footprint

Small changes, big impact - Start your sustainability journey today

🍽️ Food Tips

  • Eat less meat: Even one meatless day per week can save ~1.5 tons CO2e annually
  • Choose plant-based proteins: Lentils, beans, and tofu have 90% lower emissions
  • Buy local and seasonal: Reduces transport emissions significantly
  • Minimize food waste: Plan meals and store properly
  • Reduce dairy: Consider plant-based milk alternatives
  • Start composting: Divert food waste from landfills
  • Support sustainable farms: Look for organic and local options

⚡ Energy Tips

  • Switch to LED bulbs: Use 75% less energy than incandescent
  • Use renewable energy: Consider solar panels or green electricity plans
  • Improve insulation: Seal gaps and add weatherstripping
  • Use smart thermostats: Can save up to 15% on heating/cooling
  • Unplug devices: Standby power accounts for 10% of usage
  • Use power strips: Control multiple devices at once
  • Take shorter showers: Reduce hot water usage

🚗 Transport Tips

  • Use public transport: 5 times lower emissions than driving alone
  • Carpool or share rides: Divide emissions among passengers
  • Cycle or walk: Zero emissions and great health benefits
  • Work from home: When possible, eliminates commute emissions
  • Plan efficient routes: Reduces unnecessary mileage
  • Consider electric vehicles: 50% lower emissions over lifetime
  • Fly less: One transatlantic flight = month of driving

♻️ General Tips

  • Buy less, choose well: Reduce consumption and choose sustainable products
  • Recycle properly: Extend product lifecycles
  • Support renewable energy: Switch your utility provider if possible
  • Plant trees: Offset some of your emissions naturally
  • Spread awareness: Educate others in your community
  • Support climate policies: Vote for environmental action
  • Join communities: Connect with like-minded sustainability advocates

🏠 Home Improvements

  • Install solar panels: Generate clean energy for years
  • Upgrade appliances: Energy Star certified use 10-50% less
  • Add insulation: Reduces heating and cooling needs
  • Install water heater blanket: Easy and cheap
  • Fix leaks: Save water and reduce heating needs
  • Paint with eco-friendly paint: Lower VOC emissions
  • Use natural light: Open curtains during day

🛒 Shopping Wisely

  • Buy secondhand: Reduce manufacturing emissions
  • Choose minimal packaging: Less waste overall
  • Support eco-brands: Companies committed to sustainability
  • Buy durable goods: Last longer, less replacement
  • Avoid single-use plastics: Use reusable bags and bottles
  • Buy in bulk: Reduces packaging per item
  • Check carbon labels: Growing number on products
