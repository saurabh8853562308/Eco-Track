Understanding the Carbon Footprint Calculator

Our Mission

Most of us in places like Ghaziabad feel disconnected from the big picture of climate change, unsure how our daily lives contribute to it. Our mission is to bridge that gap by providing a simple, friendly guide to help you measure your personal carbon footprint and take meaningful action.

What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases (primarily carbon dioxide) generated by our actions. It includes direct emissions (like from heating your home) and indirect emissions (like those required to produce electricity or food).

The average global carbon footprint is around 4-5 tons CO₂e per person per year. To limit global warming to 2°C, we need to reduce this to about 2-3 tons per person annually.

How Our Calculator Works

Our calculator uses scientific data to estimate your carbon footprint across three major categories:

🍽️ Food

Different foods have vastly different carbon footprints. Beef production requires significantly more resources than vegetable farming, resulting in higher emissions.

⚡ Energy

Electricity and heating represent major sources of household emissions. The carbon intensity depends on your local energy grid.

🚗 Transport

Personal vehicles are significant carbon emitters. Public transportation, walking, and cycling offer much lower-emission alternatives.
